Why EduWash exists

More than a car wash.

Our vision

To be the most trusted service for educators in Mitchells Plain, and the first step into working life for its young people.

Our mission

To give educators back their afternoons and weekends by detailing their cars safely while they teach, and to give every matriculant on our team a paid year of skills, discipline and purpose.

Our purpose

Teachers give everything to our children, then lose their Saturdays in a car wash queue. Young people finish matric with nowhere to start. EduWash fixes both.

Every wash you book helps a young person take their first step.
